## Helm 0.1.0

The first release. Helm keeps your PC fast, clean and private, from one small window.

- **Overview:** what needs attention (disk space, junk, startup apps, privacy settings, pending restarts), live CPU, memory, GPU, disk and network with history, per-thread load, your PC's parts and every drive.
- **Cleanup:** temp files, caches, crash dumps, old update leftovers and logs, browser and app caches, shader caches and the Recycle Bin. You see each size first; files in use and apps that are open are left alone.
- **Storage:** see which folders and files use the most space, click into folders, open anything in Explorer.
- **Startup apps:** turn apps on and off the same way Task Manager does.
- **Tweaks:** 26 privacy, ad, Explorer, performance and gaming switches, each one undoable, plus power plans.
- **Tools:** restore point, system file check, DISM, disk check, network reset and more, plus links to the rest of your suite.
- **Themes:** the same 14 themes and theme files as Hangar.
- **Updates itself** with verified downloads and automatic rollback.
